Choosing between them

Which line suits which counter.

Most of this page is either warming or cooling, and that is the first question a buyer should settle. Formula 1 warms, for aches, sprains and stiff joints. Formula 2 cools, for headaches, blocked noses and insect bites. The Cheng Cheng oils sit outside that split: they are bought by name and by box colour rather than by formula. After that it is a question of format and price point.
