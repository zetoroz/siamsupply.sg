About

Who you are buying from.

Siam Supply Co. is the trade name of Jiratham Pte. Ltd., a Singapore company incorporated in August 2024 and run by Dan Ler. We import Thai household remedies and supply them to Singapore trade buyers by the carton.

Where the goods come from

We buy direct from a supplier in Hat Yai, in southern Thailand, rather than through a Singapore middleman. That is the whole basis of the pricing: there is one margin between the Thai factory gate and your shelf instead of two. Goods are supplied exactly as manufactured and packed in Thailand, with the original Thai FDA registrations and manufacturer labelling intact.

Everything we list is a brand that already sells itself. Hong Thai, Poy-Sian, Siang Pure, Pim Saen, Happy Noz, Namman Muay. We are not asking a shop to take a chance on something nobody has heard of.

How we actually work

We hold stock on the faster lines and bring the rest in on the next purchase order from Thailand. So a quote tells you which of your lines ships in about five working days and which takes up to four weeks. We would rather say that up front than promise a date we cannot hold.

Minimums follow the supplier's own carton and dozen breaks, not an artificial hurdle we invented. Payment is on delivery as standard, and Net 30 is available to community clubs, schools and corporate buyers on request.

Who buys from us

Provision shops, pharmacies and TCM halls stocking a Thai remedies shelf. Community clubs and welfare groups doing bulk goodie-bag fulfilment, which is where our largest repeat orders come from. Corporate and HR buyers doing staff wellness packs and festive gifting. Event organisers running roadshows, bazaars and marathons.

We sell trade only. If you want one or two pieces, our retail storefronts are the right place, not this desk.
