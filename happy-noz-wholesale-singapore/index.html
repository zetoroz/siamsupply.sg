Happy Noz trade questions

What is the minimum order for Happy Noz in Singapore?

200 boxes. Above the minimum you can top up one supplier pack at a time, which is 1 pieces for this line, so you are never forced to buy a round number that does not suit your shelf.

How long does delivery take?

About five working days for anything we hold in stock, and up to four weeks for anything we bring in on the next purchase order from Hat Yai. We tell you which applies when we quote, before you commit. Delivery is to your door anywhere in Singapore and the cost is already inside the quoted price.
