Thai inhalers trade questions

What is the minimum order for thai inhalers in Singapore?

120 pieces, which is the minimum on Pastel X8 Pocket Inhaler. Minimums run per line, not per order, so the cheapest way in is to start with the lowest one and add the heavier lines once you know what moves. Above the minimum you top up one supplier pack at a time.

Which thai inhalers do you supply?

15 SKUs across 6 lines: Hong Thai Compound Herb Inhaler, Poy-Sian Mark II Inhaler, Pastel X8 Pocket Inhaler, Happy Noz Nasal Stickers, Pim Saen Balm, Siang Pure Oils & Balms. All are supplied as manufactured and packed in Thailand, with the original Thai FDA registrations and manufacturer labelling intact.

Can I mix lines in one order?

Yes, and it is usually the right move. Minimums apply per line, so you can combine any lines on this page with anything else we carry to build one shipment, one quotation and one delivery.

Do you sell to the public?

No. We are a trade importer and we supply businesses only: provision shops, pharmacies and TCM halls, community clubs and welfare groups, corporate and HR buyers, event organisers and resellers. Orders start at the minimums above.
