The Hong Thai recall, in plain terms.

In October 2025 Thailand's FDA found microbial contamination in one production lot of Hong Thai Herbal Inhaler Formula 2, and the maker recalled it. It was one lot, number 000332. Here is what was found, what Singapore's HSA said, and how to check the stock on your shelf.

Last checked 28.09.2026

The recalled lot

Product
Hong Thai Brand Herbal Inhaler, Formula 2
Lot
000332 (also written as lot 332)
Made
9 December 2024
Expiry
8 December 2027
Quantity
200,000 jars
Found by
Thailand FDA, random testing, announced 28 October 2025
Finding
Total aerobic microbial count and yeast and mould count above the limit, and Clostridium bacteria present
Status
Recalled by the maker and scheduled for destruction

The maker, Thai Herbal Hong Thai Co., Ltd., said the problem was confined to lot 332 and that other batches were unaffected. It offered refunds or replacement stock to trade partners and said it would sterilise every future batch, working with the Thailand Institute of Nuclear Technology.

Questions retailers ask

Which Hong Thai products were recalled?

One production lot of one product: Hong Thai Brand Herbal Inhaler Formula 2, lot 000332, made on 9 December 2024 with an expiry of 8 December 2027. The recall was by lot, not by formula, so Formula 2 jars from other lots were not part of it.

Is Hong Thai legal to sell in Singapore?

Yes. HSA said that, going by its labelling, the inhaler is considered a traditional medicine and is not subject to HSA approval or licensing for import, manufacture or sale. HSA's action on the recall was to advise anyone holding lot 000332 to stop using it and to ask Shopee, Lazada and Carousell to remove listings of that lot.

Is the Hong Thai you supply from the recalled lot?

No. None of the Hong Thai we supply is from lot 000332. If you want to see it for yourself, ask and we will tell you the lot number of the stock on your order before you pay.

How do I check a jar?

The lot number and the manufacture and expiry dates are printed on the jar and its box. Look for lot 000332, or the pairing of a 9 December 2024 manufacture date with an 8 December 2027 expiry. Anything else was not part of the recall.

What about the factory raids?

On 30 October 2025 Thai police and the FDA raided production sites linked to the brand, after finding the company was licensed to produce at one site in Bangkok's Bang Khae district but had been producing at three other sites in Samut Sakhon without approval. Over two million inhalers and balms were seized. That was about where the goods were made, which is why buying from a supplier who can name the lot and issue a proper invoice matters.

Did Malaysia ban Hong Thai?

Malaysia's health ministry said on 6 November 2025 that Hong Thai Formula 2 is not registered with its pharmaceutical regulator and that selling unregistered medicines is an offence there. That is Malaysian law. Singapore treats the product as a traditional medicine, as above.
